## 2. Forecasting models — real training, real evaluation

Protocol: per-series chronological split 70/15/15 (no shuffling, ever);
z-score fitted on the train segment only; neural models trained on pooled
train windows (Online Retail + Superstore), early-stopped on pooled
validation; evaluated both one-step-ahead and on a recursive 30-day
forecast from the test boundary. Selection metric: pooled recursive-30-day
scaled test MAE (matches the production task).

### Pooled test metrics (z-scaled units)

| Model | 1-step MAE | 1-step RMSE | 1-step R² | 30-day recursive MAE | RMSE | R² |
|-------|-----------|------------|-----------|----------------------|------|-----|
| RNN (NumPy BPTT) | 1.0166 | 1.4821 | −0.0561 | 0.8053 | 1.0060 | 0.1371 |
| LSTM (NumPy BPTT) | 1.0578 | 1.5122 | −0.0994 | 0.9078 | 1.0474 | 0.0646 |
| **GRU (NumPy BPTT)** | 0.9880 | 1.4707 | −0.0400 | **0.7512** | 0.9276 | **0.2664** |
| AR(14) pooled OLS | **0.9599** | **1.4087** | 0.0460 | 0.8746 | 1.0567 | 0.0479 |
| Naive last-value | 1.3178 | 1.7706 | −0.5072 | 1.3612 | 1.8338 | −1.8675 |
| Moving average (7) | 1.0416 | 1.4197 | 0.0310 | 0.8581 | 1.0237 | 0.1065 |

**Selected: GRU** — best at the production task (30-day recursive forecast).
Honest nuance: AR(14) is the best *one-step* predictor, but degrades faster
over a 30-day horizon; the deep models' advantage appears exactly in
multi-step forecasting. On Superstore daily sales all models are weak
(noisy day-level data; naive is competitive) — reported as-is, no
cherry-picking.

### Phase 14 — did more datasets help? (honest answer)

GRU trained on retail-only vs pooled (same test rows):

| Test series | Retail-only training | Pooled training | Verdict |
|-------------|---------------------|-----------------|---------|
| Online Retail 30-day MAE | 0.8086 | 0.7774 | pooling **improved** |
| Superstore 30-day MAE | 0.6508 | 0.7250 | pooling **degraded** |

Net effect ≈ neutral (selected pooled GRU 0.7512 vs retail-only average
0.7297). **More datasets did not automatically improve the model** —
pooling broadened coverage at a small cost on Superstore. Reported honestly.

## 3. Fraud / anomaly models

Protocol: chronological splits (PaySim by step, ULB by exact Time
boundaries so identical rows never straddle splits); scaler fitted on
train only; F1 thresholds tuned on validation, never test; row-identity
duplicate audits PASS for both datasets (0 raw records span splits;
feature collisions — distinct transactions that merely look alike — are
counted and documented as informational).

| Model | Dataset | Precision | Recall | F1 | PR-AUC | ROC-AUC |
|-------|---------|-----------|--------|-----|--------|---------|
| finmind_fraud_paysim (HGB, supervised) | PaySim | 0.800 | 0.927 | 0.859 | 0.960 | 0.998 |
| finmind_fraud_ulb (Logistic, supervised) | ULB | 0.120 | 0.815 | 0.209 | 0.716 | 0.977 |
| finmind_fraud_ulb_hgb (HGB, supervised) | ULB | 0.127 | 0.833 | 0.221 | 0.754 | 0.944 |
| finmind_anomaly (IsolationForest, live schema) | PaySim | 0.124 | 0.014 | 0.026 | 0.075 | 0.796 |
| finmind_fraud_ulb_iforest (IF on train normals) | ULB | 0.016 | 0.019 | 0.017 | 0.046 | 0.949 |

**Old artifact vs new, on identical chronological ULB test rows:**
`iforest_fraud_v1` (as shipped, with its own leaky preprocessing) scored
**F1 0.0 / PR-AUC 0.066** — its previously-claimed F1 0.117 was inflated by
a random split that leaked fitted rows into evaluation. The new supervised
models reach PR-AUC 0.716–0.754 on the same rows.

**finmind_anomaly** (unsupervised, generic features) is honestly weak as a
fraud detector — that is expected for an unsupervised screening prior. In
the live service its flags are always confirmed by per-business statistical
evidence (z-score / multiple-of-median / off-hours / duplicates), and it
replaces the old artifact which was never usable live at all (PCA schema
mismatch) *and* was fitted during every request.

## 5. Reproducibility

- Random seed: 42 (all training); full-run determinism verified by test
  (`test_seed_reproducibility`).
- Hyperparameters (forecasting): seq_len 14, hidden 16, Adam lr 0.03
  (β1 0.9, β2 0.999, eps 1e-8), gradient clip ±5, max 150 epochs,
  patience 15, full-batch, MSE loss. Early stopping restored best
  weights: RNN 30 epochs (best 15), LSTM 43 (best 28), GRU 61 (best 46).
- Environment: Python 3.9, NumPy 2.0.2, pandas 2.3.3, scikit-learn 1.6.1,
  statsmodels 0.14.6, SciPy 1.13.1 (recorded per registry entry).
- Artifacts: `data/models/finmind_*/` (model + config + metrics) with
  SHA-256 checksums in `data/models/registry.json`; checksums re-verified
  on load at inference and by test.

## 6. Inference architecture (no training at request time)

```
business daily series (PostgreSQL)
  → validation (≥60 days)
  → z-score with the business's own observed history
  → registry-selected artifact (checksum-verified, loaded once)
  → recursive multi-step forecast
  → inverse transform → MYR payload with provenance
```

Gemini is NOT trained. It calls the trained models through the existing
controlled tools (`get_forecast`, `get_anomalies`) — unchanged contracts,
real values only.
